Background

Parkinson's disease

A progressive neurological disorder that affects movement. Common motor signs include resting tremor, rigidity, and slowed, diminished motion (bradykinesia).

Handwriting and spiral drawing externalize that motor control loss: strokes slow unevenly, correct themselves mid-line, and accelerate in small jerks a healthy hand rarely produces. This demo scores those motion patterns directly — not the shape you end up with.

Not a diagnosis This is a personal research project, not a medical device. It cannot confirm or rule out Parkinson's disease.
